# Editorial QA Checklist (Phase 2)

Use this checklist before importing or syncing lesson content to runtime.

## Doctrine and Scope

- Confirm lesson objective is explicit and matches the phase plan.
- Confirm doctrinal frame is declared when terms are ambiguous.
- Confirm classical and modern definitions are separated where needed.
- Confirm no deterministic life-outcome claims are presented as certainty.

## Learner Safety and Trust

- Confirm wording avoids fear-amplifying or fatalistic statements.
- Confirm strategic framing: interpretation leads to better decisions, not rigid fate.
- Confirm examples do not rely on gender-essentialist claims.

## Structure and Pedagogy

- Confirm progression goes from concept to method to application.
- Confirm at least one section covers common mistakes or misdiagnosis.
- Confirm one-minute takeaway exists and matches lesson objective.

## Assessment Quality

- Confirm question bank tests understanding, not only lookup recall.
- Confirm at least one diagnostic or sequence item exists in each bank where relevant.
- Confirm hints and explanations are consistent with lesson wording.
- Confirm duplicate prompts and contradictory answers are removed.

## Terminology and Consistency

- Confirm key terms are used consistently across steps, banks, and html content.
- Confirm title, subtitle, and positioning (main path vs supplemental) are aligned.
- Confirm transliteration or Chinese term variants are normalized.

## Runtime Readiness

- Run content validation.
- Run lesson-level balance audit when required by rollout acceptance criteria.
- Sync to runtime DB and verify question counts match expected totals.
- Record audit output path and sync command in release notes.
