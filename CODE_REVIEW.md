# Editorial Audit: BaZi Game Curriculum

## Scope

This audit executes item 1 of the rollout plan:

- classify each lesson as `core`, `advanced`, or `lineage-specific`
- define outcome-based learning objectives for lesson 5 and lesson 9
- make a product decision on lesson 10

The goal is to turn the current content set into a clearer learning path, reduce doctrinal ambiguity, and prepare the course for later content and quiz rewrites.

## Classification Rules

### `core`

Content every learner should complete to understand BaZi basics and follow later lessons.

### `advanced`

Content that is still structurally important, but assumes the learner already understands the foundational order of analysis and can tolerate more technical vocabulary.

### `lineage-specific`

Content that may be valuable, but reflects a particular school, interpretive lens, or controversial framing and should not be taught as universal baseline doctrine.

## Lesson 5 Audit: 十神詳解

### Current status

Lesson 5 has the right conceptual backbone. It already frames ten gods as a relationship system rather than a fixed personality label, which is the correct direction for a serious beginner curriculum. It also introduces good later-stage ideas such as combination reading, placement, strength, and strategic use.

The main weakness is assessment balance. Too much of the bank is spent on symbol lookup and direct mapping. That builds recognition speed, but not enough interpretive judgment.

### Desired role in curriculum

Lesson 5 should be the learner's transition from `knowing names` to `reading functions`.

### Learning objectives

By the end of lesson 5, the learner should be able to:

1. identify the ten-god category of a stem relative to the day master by using five-element relationship first and yin-yang second
2. explain why ten gods are relational functions, not fixed good-or-bad labels
3. distinguish single-symbol lookup from actual reading by checking strength, placement, rooting, and interaction
4. recognize core structural combinations such as 官印相生, 食神制殺, 傷官見官, and 財星壞印 as relationship patterns rather than isolated slogans
5. state a cautious interpretation using language of tendency, structure, and context instead of deterministic personality labeling

### What lesson 5 should explicitly teach

- ten gods are defined relative to the day master
- the correct order is: relation type, yin-yang split, strength, position, rooting, then interaction
- ten gods do not carry fixed destiny judgments outside full-chart context
- a ten-god reading is only meaningful once it is placed back into the whole chart structure

### What lesson 5 should stop doing

- over-relying on repetitive stem-to-ten-god flashcard questions
- implying that one ten god maps to one personality destiny conclusion
- treating lookup fluency as equivalent to interpretive competence

## Lesson 9 Audit: 格局取用：月令與古典用神

### Current status

Lesson 9 captures the right doctrinal skeleton: month branch priority, heaven-stem emergence from month-branch storage, main qi priority, basic categories of patterns, and the distinction between classical `用神` and modern `喜用神` language.

The main weakness is that the learner is told the framework, but is not yet trained to perform it procedurally.

### Desired role in curriculum

Lesson 9 should function as the learner's first real classical diagnostic workflow lesson.

### Learning objectives

By the end of lesson 9, the learner should be able to:

1. explain why month branch qi is the first checkpoint in classical pattern judgment
2. distinguish classical `用神` as the chart's working pivot from modern balancing-language about `喜用神`
3. follow a stable sequence for pattern judgment: inspect month branch, inspect hidden stems, check emergence, prioritize main qi, then test whether the structure is supported or broken
4. tell the difference between naming a pattern and proving that the pattern is actually formed
5. identify common break conditions in a simple example, especially for 正官格 and 七殺格

### What lesson 9 should explicitly teach

- month branch is the diagnostic entry point, not one data point among many
- `天透地藏` and `本氣為主` are not trivia terms; they are decision rules
- pattern naming is only step one
- the next questions are whether the day master can bear the pattern and whether the rest of the chart protects or damages it
- `成格`, `破格`, and `清濁` should be taught as evaluation stages, not decorative jargon

### What lesson 9 should stop doing

- stopping at label recognition
- letting learners confuse classical pattern doctrine with general modern balancing talk
- presenting pattern names without worked diagnosis sequences

## Lesson 10 Decision

### Decision

`Relabel and move off the default learning path.`

### Why this decision

The current lesson 10 is not suitable as baseline curriculum in its current form. Its framing is too direct and too universalizing for claims that are better treated as lineage-bound interpretation. The present version ties `陽體/陰體` directly to male/female and then maps certain structures to those bodies as if the fit were doctrinally settled. That is too strong for the main course.

### Product handling

- remove lesson 10 from the default progression path
- relabel it as a supplemental module
- add an introductory notice that it presents one interpretive framework rather than universally accepted beginner doctrine

### Editorial handling

If kept, lesson 10 should be rewritten around these rules:

1. present `體用` as one framework among several, not as the only correct lens
2. avoid deterministic male/female suitability claims as baseline teaching
3. replace categorical statements with lineage framing such as `某些古典師承會這樣理解`
4. move from identity-based judgment to interpretive-fit language where possible
5. make clear that this module is interpretive and comparative, not foundational literacy

### Recommended replacement label

Use one of:

- `第10課：體用觀的流派補充`
- `第10課：古典體用觀補充`
- `第10課：體用觀的特定師承解法`

## Immediate Editorial Actions

1. Mark lessons 0-5 as the mandatory beginner foundation in product copy and navigation.
2. Mark lessons 6-9 and 11 as advanced modules that still belong to the official curriculum.
3. Move lesson 10 into a supplemental category before further public-facing polishing.
4. Rewrite lesson 5 quizzes around interpretation, not just mapping.
5. Rewrite lesson 9 around workflow and worked examples, not just summary bullets.
